01 Who We Are

This Privacy Policy is published by Society Revolution, the developer and operator of Eco Transformacion Pipeline ("Application", "we", "us", or "our").

Eco Transformacion Pipeline is a TikTok API-integrated tool that enables authorized users to manage and publish content to TikTok. We are not affiliated with TikTok Inc. or ByteDance Ltd.

02 Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to Eco Transformacion Pipeline and governs how we collect, process, store, and protect information when you use this Application. It does not apply to TikTok's own services — please review TikTok's Privacy Policy for information about how TikTok handles your data.

03 Information We Collect

Eco Transformacion Pipeline collects the minimum information necessary to provide its functionality. The categories of data we may access or process are:

Data Type Source Purpose
TikTok OAuth access token TikTok authentication flow To authenticate API requests on your behalf
TikTok user ID and display name TikTok API (with your authorization) To identify your account session within the Application
Video metadata (title, description, tags) Content you submit through the Application To prepare and publish content to your TikTok account
Video files Files you upload through the Application To upload and publish videos to your TikTok account; files are not retained after upload
Application usage logs Generated during your use of the Application Error diagnosis and service improvement; no personally identifiable information is logged

04 How We Use Your Information

Eco Transformacion Pipeline uses the information described above solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your session with TikTok's API
  • Execute the publishing and content management actions you explicitly initiate
  • Display confirmation of actions performed on your behalf
  • Diagnose and fix technical errors within the Application
  • Improve the functionality and performance of the Application
All actions performed by Eco Transformacion Pipeline are initiated and explicitly authorized by you. The Application does not perform automated, background, or scheduled actions without your direct consent.

05 Data We Do Not Collect

Eco Transformacion Pipeline does NOT:
  • Sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal data to any third party
  • Use your data for advertising, profiling, or behavioral tracking
  • Access the TikTok accounts, content, or data of other users
  • Store payment, financial, or government-issued identity information
  • Conduct surveillance, analytics, or monitoring of your device or online activities
  • Engage in mass data collection or scraping of TikTok content

06 Data Sharing and Disclosure

Society Revolution does not share your personal information with third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • TikTok API: Your content and credentials are transmitted to TikTok via their official API solely to execute the actions you request. This transmission is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Protection of rights: We may disclose information to protect the rights, safety, or property of Society Revolution, its users, or the public.

07 Data Retention

Eco Transformacion Pipeline retains data only for as long as necessary to provide the service:

  • OAuth tokens: Retained only for the duration of your active session. Revoked upon logout.
  • Video files: Not stored by the Application after successful upload to TikTok.
  • Content metadata: Retained only as long as needed to confirm publishing; not archived.
  • Application logs: Retained for up to 30 days for debugging purposes, then deleted.

You may request immediate deletion of any data associated with your use of Eco Transformacion Pipeline at any time by contacting us at societyrevolution4@gmail.com.

08 Data Security

Society Revolution implements reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information processed by Eco Transformacion Pipeline against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between the Application and TikTok's API
  • OAuth 2.0 authentication — the Application never handles or stores your TikTok password
  • Minimal data access — the Application requests only the TikTok API scopes strictly necessary for its functionality

While we take reasonable precautions, no security system is impenetrable. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

09 Your Rights

With respect to data processed by Eco Transformacion Pipeline, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of personal data the Application holds about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your data. We will comply within 30 days of receiving a verifiable request.
  • Revocation: Revoke the Application's access to your TikTok account at any time via TikTok's App Permissions settings at tiktok.com/setting.
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format where technically feasible.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: societyrevolution4@gmail.com

10 Third-Party Services

Eco Transformacion Pipeline integrates exclusively with the TikTok API as its third-party platform. The Application does not embed analytics trackers, advertising networks, or other third-party data processors.

TikTok's handling of your data within their platform is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, which are independent of this document.

11 Children's Privacy

Eco Transformacion Pipeline is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect or process personal data from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, contact us immediately at societyrevolution4@gmail.com and we will delete such data promptly.

12 Changes to This Privacy Policy

Society Revolution may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.

Your continued use of Eco Transformacion Pipeline after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
